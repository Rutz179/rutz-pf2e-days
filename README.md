# Rutz's PF2e Days

Plan how the party spends its days: drag activities onto each character's waking hours and advance as many days as you like.

- **Day bar** — Daily Preparations, Craft, Earn Income, rituals, training, Subsist, watch… plans stay until changed.
- **Advance** — +1 / +3 / +7 / N days: the world clock moves and the chat card shows who did what. Staying up too long leaves you fatigued.
- **Checks** — Earn Income (paid by the rules, GM multiplier) and Subsist cards.
- **Plus** — lodging, meals and cost of living (night card, food buffs, hunger checks), travel planner (distance, terrain, mounts, fares), automatic Earn Income requests, time declarations.

## Lite & Plus
This is the free edition; the **Plus** features above come with a Patreon membership (same module, so worlds keep their data). During the open beta every feature is free.

```
https://github.com/Rutz179/rutz-pf2e-days/releases/latest/download/module.json
```
